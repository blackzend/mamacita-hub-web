Privacy policy

Updated November 22, 2026. Mamacita's Hub is a private family application maintained by Kevin.

Google contacts

When authorized, the app reads names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, birthdays, organizations, notes, photos, links and favorites to display your directory and communication shortcuts. Access is read-only; it does not modify Google contacts.

Storage

Google authorization tokens, cached contacts and downloaded photos are stored locally, encrypted for your Windows account. Settings and communication preferences are stored locally. Contacts and tokens are not uploaded to Kevin's website or the public tips feed.

External services

When you request AI help, your question and recent conversation context are sent to Google Gemini. Your contact directory is not automatically included. Keep passwords and bank details out of questions. Opening Gmail, WhatsApp or Teams passes the destination and, when applicable, your prepared message to that service. You review and send in the destination application. Windows dictation operates under Windows privacy settings.

Control and deletion

The app does not sell Google data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Revoke access through your Google Account's third-party connections page. Ask Kevin to remove the app's local configuration folder to delete cached contacts, photos, preferences and authorization. Generic tips can be downloaded from a public shared file; personal contacts are not published there.

Contact

For privacy, support or deletion assistance, contact Kevin through Message Kevin or the support email displayed by Google during authorization.
